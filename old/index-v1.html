Our Story

Why we started North & Noble Care

North & Noble Care began with a simple, familiar struggle: watching someone we loved need more help than we could give alone, and not knowing who to trust with something so personal.

We built the kind of care company we wished had existed then — one where caregivers are chosen as carefully as you'd choose them for your own family, where communication is constant instead of occasional, and where "care" means showing up with patience, not just a checklist.

Today, our team supports families across [PLACEHOLDER: SERVICE AREA] with the same promise we started with: your loved one is treated like family, because to us, they are.

How We Help

Care that fits your family's life

Every family's situation is different, so our services are built to flex — from a few hours of weekly companionship to full-time, around-the-clock support. Tap "Learn more" on any service for details.

Personal Care

Help with the everyday routines that keep your loved one comfortable, clean, and dignified — bathing, grooming, dressing, and getting around safely.

Our caregivers assist with bathing and hygiene, grooming and dressing, toileting, mobility and transfers, and safe positioning — always with patience and respect for your loved one's privacy and preferences.

Companionship Care

Friendly conversation, shared meals, favorite hobbies, and someone warm to spend the day with — because connection matters as much as care.

Loneliness can be as hard on health as any physical condition. Our companion caregivers play cards, go for walks, cook together, read aloud, and simply keep your loved one engaged and smiling.

Respite Care

A trusted break for family caregivers. Whether you need a few hours or a few days, we step in so you can rest, recharge, or run errands in peace.

Caring for a parent or spouse is a full-time act of love — and it's exhausting. Our respite caregivers step in for planned breaks, vacations, or just a quiet evening, giving you permission to care for yourself without guilt.

Dementia & Alzheimer's Care

Specially trained caregivers who understand memory loss, use gentle redirection, and create calm, familiar routines that ease confusion and anxiety.

Our team receives specific training in dementia and Alzheimer's care: communicating clearly, de-escalating agitation, supporting safe wandering prevention, and maintaining consistent routines that preserve dignity and moments of joy.

Post-Hospital & Recovery Care

Extra hands during the critical weeks after a hospital stay or surgery, helping your loved one heal safely at home and avoid a return trip.

We support medication schedules, follow discharge instructions, assist with mobility reminders, prepare nourishing meals, and watch for warning signs — working alongside your medical team for a smooth, safe recovery.

Palliative & End-of-Life Support

Gentle, compassionate comfort care for your loved one, and emotional support for your whole family through one of life's hardest chapters.

Our caregivers provide comfort-focused support alongside hospice or palliative teams — assistance with daily needs, a calming presence, and respite for family members, so no one has to walk this road alone.

Overnight & 24-Hour Live-In Care

Around-the-clock peace of mind — whether that's overnight safety checks or a dedicated live-in companion for continuous support.

For families worried about falls, wandering, or nighttime needs, we offer overnight shifts and full live-in arrangements with consistent caregivers who learn your loved one's routines, preferences, and needs inside and out.

Light Housekeeping, Meals & Errands

A tidy home and a full fridge go a long way. We handle light cleaning, home-cooked meals, laundry, and grocery runs.

From wiping counters and washing dishes to planning meals around dietary needs and picking up prescriptions or groceries, our caregivers help keep the home running smoothly.

Medication Reminders

Friendly, consistent reminders to take the right medication at the right time — so missed doses aren't a worry you carry alone.

While our caregivers don't administer medication, they provide reliable reminders, help organize pill packs, and track adherence so you always know your loved one's routine is being followed.

Transportation & Appointment Escort

Safe rides to appointments, errands, or social visits, with a caregiver who stays for the visit and helps you follow up on what was discussed.

Missing appointments, or driving when it's no longer safe, shouldn't be the alternative. Our caregivers provide door-to-door transportation and stay by your loved one's side during visits.
